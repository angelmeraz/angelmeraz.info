Contractually, I'm not allowed to talk about the products I worked on, only the ones that are open-source:

During my time at iTech, I was presented with the opportunity, and received support, to create and maintain my first open-source project: <a href="https://www.npmjs.com/package/classgroup" target="_blank">ClassGroup</a>.

<a href="https://www.npmjs.com/package/classgroup" target="_blank">ClassGroup</a>

ClassGroup is a utility to help keep your CSS classes in JS consistently and semantically grouped while allowing for a separation of concerns. Inspired from the esteemed <a href="https://cube.fyi/grouping.html#grouping-order" target="_blank">CUBE CSS</a> methodology, it simplifies and enhances styles management and thus improving Developer Experience.

Find more about it <a href="https://github.com/itech-media/classgroup" target="_blank">here</a>.

While ClassGroup may seem straightforward, its utility has proven invaluable in addressing the challenges of theming and building a versatile, reusable component library. I take pride in how we managed to use it at scale as it helped to streamline our product development process and enhanced the overall DX.
